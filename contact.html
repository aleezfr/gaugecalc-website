Contact GaugeCalc

Found a formula that doesn't match what you see in the field? Want a calculator that doesn't exist yet? Send it over. This site is maintained by one working technician, not a support team — replies take a little time, but every message gets read.

Email: hello@gaugecalc.com — or use the feedback form to report a specific calculator issue.

What to include

For tool corrections: which calculator, what input values you used, and what you expected vs. what you got. For new tool requests: what it should calculate and why it'd be useful on a real job.

Before you write in

Every calculator's guide section (below the results) explains the formula and assumptions used — worth a quick check in case the answer is already there. All figures on this site are estimates for planning purposes; always confirm critical specs against your equipment's own datasheet before ordering hardware.
